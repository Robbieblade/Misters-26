WELCOME TO WALES

The Misters tour alternates between the UK and Europe, and after the drama of the Algarve it's the UK's turn to host. Three rounds of links golf on the North Wales coast — Porthmadog, Nefyn & District and Conwy — with Tournament HQ in the shadow of Harlech Castle. Dragon country. Bring a jumper.

Portugal came down to the final green. Two points in it. Every team has rebranded, the wildcard has been signed, and the scores are back to zero.

Before We Go

THE DRAWS

Two draws happen before we travel: the order in which teams pick their Split Round, and the order in which they pick their Brettini Round. The draws signify the start of the hype train — and there are some additions to properly level up the hype game this year. All will be revealed soon.

Friday night at HQ also hosts the 26/27 Fantasy Football Draft — Bidding Wars. Bring your budget and your grudges.
