Day One

THE ITINERARY

FRIDAY 28 AUGUST Travel · Porthmadog · Round 1 · 14:00 Tee

The Plan

  • 09:00 Depart Sheffield
  • 09:30 Depart Ruddington
  • 13:00 Arrive Porthmadog Golf Club, Morfa Bychan
  • 14:00 Round 1 tees off
  • ~18:30 Finish, then on to Aelfor for check-in

The Route

Sheffield / Ruddington → Porthmadog Golf Club → Aelfor, Harlech. Clubs in first, questions later.

Friday Evening — Harlech

Castle Cottage Inn ★★★★★

Probably the best all-round choice. Excellent food (one of the highest-rated in Harlech), a great selection of Welsh ales, and a traditional stone pub with plenty of character.

Best for: dinner and a few pints
The Lion Inn at Harlech House

Small, traditional local, recently restored with a cosy interior and outdoor decking. Local craft beers and Welsh gins. More proper pub than restaurant.

Best for: a relaxed pint before heading elsewhere
The Queen's Hotel

Friendly local atmosphere, decent beer garden if the Welsh weather behaves, and popular with locals for pub food.

Best for: casual drinks and food
SATURDAY 29 AUGUST Nefyn & District · Round 2 · 11:10 Tee

The Plan

  • AM HQ → Nefyn & District Golf Club, Morfa Nefyn
  • 11:10 Round 2 tees off — Front nine, then out onto The Point
  • NOTE The Point is confirmed. Par 71, 6,309 off the yellows — the second longest card of the tour
  • POST Drinks at the Tŷ Coch Inn, on the beach below the course
  • EVE Back to HQ, then taxi into Porthmadog

Saturday Evening — Porthmadog

The Australia ★★★★★

Top recommendation. Home of Purple Moose Brewery beers, fantastic atmosphere, and a steak & ale pie with a serious reputation. Big outdoor seating area if the weather is kind.

Perfect for: your first post-golf pint
Tafarn Pencei

Harbour-side spot with lovely views, a huge range of Welsh beers and a modern gastropub feel. Excellent burgers.

Perfect for: a relaxed meal overlooking the harbour
Spooner's Pub

Inside the historic harbour railway station — home of the Ffestiniog & Welsh Highland Railways. Good value food and plenty of room for a big group.

Perfect for: beers while watching the heritage trains
Y Llong (The Ship Inn)

Traditional Welsh pub, friendly atmosphere, very good pub classics. Dog friendly.

Perfect for: a quieter meal away from the harbour
Red Lion

Classic locals' pub with a pool table and live sport. Friendly and informal.

Perfect for: if the group just wants beers rather than food

Note: return taxi at closing — there are no late options, so nobody gets left admiring the harbour.

SUNDAY 30 AUGUST Conwy · Final Round · 11:03 Tee

The Plan

  • AM HQ → Conwy Golf Club, Beacons Way
  • 11:03 The final round. Trophies, medals and the Birdie Club pot all decided here.

Sunday Evening — The Decision…

OPTION 1

Everyone departs home.

Officially rated: worst option.

OPTION 2

Back to HQ for the awards ceremony, local pubs and final hot tub actions.

Officially rated: good option.

OPTION 3 — THE BIG ONE

Two cars go to Conwy carrying all clubs, all seven participants and evening attire. Post-golf: shower and change at the clubhouse, then a ten-minute drive to Llandudno — a large selection of pubs and later bars.

Taxis or minibus back to HQ (possibly ~£120 per taxi, needs checking — roughly £35 a man). At check-out, the remaining drivers shuttle everyone to the cars and we head home. It's en route for the Sheffield contingent, so no detour.

MONDAY 31 AUGUST Departure
  • 10:00 Check-out. Hot tub off, trophies packed, dragons appeased. Home.
