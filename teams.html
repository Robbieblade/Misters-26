THE FIELD

Three teams, seven players and a wildcard who belongs to nobody. Portugal came down to a single point on the final green, and everyone has spent the year since either defending that result or plotting against it.

What follows is a profile of every man on the Dragon Tour: what they do with a golf club, what the numbers actually say, and what they are like once the clubs go back in the boot. The scoring data is taken from the Misters 25 cards, so where a reputation and a scorecard disagree, both are printed.

THE PAIRINGS

Better Ball Stableford rewards a particular thing: not brilliance, but the absence of wasted holes. A team needs one man to post a counting score while the other hunts. On that measure the three pairings are constructed very differently.

ROB IS THE MOST CONSISTENT SCORER

Twelve points clear on best ball contribution and nearly double anyone else’s par count. Any analysis that does not start there is wrong.

STEVE IS THE MOST UNDERVALUED

Second in the field on best ball points, and the only player who never dropped below 29 in a round. The fluke narrative does not survive contact with his card.

ADAM IS THE BIGGEST MOVER

Statistically the toughest week of anyone in Portugal, but the tightest score range in the field and three shots cut since. The highest floor off the biggest handicap.

SAM IS THE WIDEST VARIANCE

The best single round of the tournament and the worst, from the same player, four days apart.

THE HANDICAP STORY

Nine shots have come off the Championdogs and five off the Sultans, while the Mailin Men have lost three. John is the only man in the field playing off exactly what he played off last year. In a format decided by a single point across 54 holes, that redistribution is more significant than any swing change anyone has made over the winter.

Three questions for the Dragon Tour

  • Does Sam actually hit the 5-iron off the tee, and does it hold for three rounds?
  • Is the improved Adam the real Adam, or a good six months?
  • Can Phil produce the Silves version of himself more than once now that six shots have gone?
