WHEN CAN YOU GO?

Mark up the calendar below for 17 May to 1 September 2027. Pick a colour, then tap or drag across the days. It takes about a minute.

RED — CAN'T DO IT

Wedding, holiday, work trip, anything immovable. If it's red, we won't book it.

AMBER — DOABLE

Would take some negotiating, but you could make it work if it's the only option.

GREEN — GOOD

No problem at all. The more green, the easier this gets.

TWO OBVIOUS WINDOWS — BE HONEST ABOUT THESE

The bank holiday weekends are Fri 28 – Mon 31 May and Fri 27 – Mon 30 August. The August one is the straight repeat of the Dragon Tour. They're outlined in gold on the calendar.

They're also the two most expensive and most booked-up weekends of the summer, so if a normal weekend works just as well for you, say so.
