TWO THINGS THE LISTING FLAGS

No tumble dryer. Three coastal links courses in late August and only a washing machine. If Friday at Porthmadog is wet, Saturday morning starts damp unless someone thinks ahead. Second pair of shoes, spare glove, and use the heating overnight.

No oven or hob is listed, and no barbecue. If the barbecue idea is happening, someone brings one. Worth checking with Sykes rather than assuming the listing is just incomplete.

The Thing Nobody Mentioned

ROYAL ST DAVID'S — 0.7 KM

WE HAVE BOOKED A HOUSE NEXT DOOR TO A CHAMPIONSHIP LINKS

The Sykes listing puts Royal St David's Golf Club an eight minute walk from the front door, and lists the property as being next to a golf course. Royal St David's is one of the great links courses in Wales, laid out beneath Harlech Castle among the dunes, and a regular Open Final Qualifying venue.

It is not on the itinerary. Three questions follow from that:

  • Can we get on for a fourth round, or a bonus nine, given we are staying next door?
  • Is there a practice ground or putting green we can use on the Friday or Saturday evening?
  • If it is beyond us on price or availability, is it worth a walk up for a pint in the clubhouse and a look at the first tee?

Someone should ring them. Green fees at a course of that standing in late August will not be cheap and a Bank Holiday weekend is the worst possible time to ask, but the walk from the house is shorter than the walk from most car parks to most first tees.

Do we book a grocery delivery for breakfasts and snacks? Three mornings with 14:00, 11:10 and 11:03 tees means breakfast at HQ every day rather than on the road.

Bear in mind the kitchen list runs to a fridge, dishwasher, toaster and kettle. No oven, hob or microwave appears on it. Plan the shop around that, or confirm with Sykes first. There is no barbecue listed either.

Someone needs to own this. Shout up in the group.
