Field average is points per player. Nefyn was comfortably the hardest day of the three, and the round that decided the tour.

PORTHMADOG

The shortest card but the most demanding walk, with the most elevation of the three. Stroke index 1 and 3 come back to back at the 14th and 15th.

Of note: Rob's gross birdie at the 7th — a four at a par 5 for four points.

NEFYN & DISTRICT

Clifftop golf on the Llŷn, playing Front and The Point. Only three par 3s and two par 5s in eighteen, and stroke index 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 are all on the back nine.

Of note: Rob's 84 for 38 points, on a day the field averaged 24.4.

CONWY

The longest card of the tour and a genuine championship links. Three par 5s in the five holes from the 10th to the 14th.

Of note: Steve's gross two at the 6th, the lowest score anyone made all week.
