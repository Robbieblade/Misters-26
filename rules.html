How It Works

RULES & FORMAT

With Cox once again proving that No Show isn't just a nickname, the format has been rethought. Six players, three teams, and one free agent riding with a different pairing every day.

The Competition

BETTER BALL STABLEFORD

Three rounds of 18 holes. Better Ball Stableford with your partner throughout. The only change from previous years is the two draws that happen before we tee off.

DRAW 1 — THE SPLIT ROUND

Each team picks one of the three rounds where they split up and play in different groups. You're still playing Better Ball Stableford with your partner — you're just not walking round together for that round.

A draw decides the order in which teams choose their preferred split round.

DRAW 2 — THE BRETTINI ROUND

Each team also picks one round where they're joined by The Great Brettini. For that round your Better Ball becomes a three-score format, with the best score on each hole counting for the team.

A separate draw decides the order in which teams pick their Brettini round.

BOTH DRAWS HAPPEN BEFORE WE TRAVEL

Everyone will know their split round and their Brettini round before we head off. The draws signify the start of the hype train — and there are some additions to properly level up the hype game this year. All will be revealed soon.

The Wildcard

THE GREAT BRETTINI

Brett Miller. The Lone Wolf belongs to no team. Instead, he's golf's answer to a free agent — playing with a different team each day across the three rounds, giving that team a third score to choose from on every hole. A random draw decides which team gets Brett on which day.

BRETTINI PERFORMANCE RELATED PAY™

To keep things fair, Brett only gets rewarded if he genuinely contributes. The winning team is treated as having three players, and one player's share of the prize money is reserved as Brett's maximum possible payout.

We then count how many holes the winning team actually used Brett's score:

The Payout Scale
Holes where Brett's score countedBrett receives
0 holes0% of a player's share
9 holes50% of a player's share
All 18 holes100% of a player's share

Whatever Brett earns comes out of that reserved share, with the remaining prize money split equally between the two permanent members of the winning team.

In short: if Brett carries you, he gets paid. If he spends the day admiring your golf, he doesn't.

The Pot

PRIZE MONEY

£30 EACH

Everyone pays £30, which covers:

  • Overall winners' prize
  • Medals
  • Daily competitions

DAILY COMPETITIONS

  • Closest to the Pin on every par 3
  • Closest in 3 on every par 5
THE BIRDIE CLUB

£3 from every entry goes into the Birdie Club. At the end of the three days:

  • If there are only birdies, the pot is split equally between everyone who made one.
  • If anyone makes an eagle, they swoop in and take the entire Birdie Club pot.
