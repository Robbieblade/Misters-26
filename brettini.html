THE GREAT BRETTINI

Brett Miller (18)
The Lone Wolf

He belongs to nobody. Over the three rounds Brett plays with a different team each day, giving that team a third score to choose from on every hole, with the draw deciding who gets him when. Whether he gets paid depends entirely on how often his score is the one that counts.

He arrives, it should be said, having been let down. The other half of last year’s no-shows has no-showed again, leaving Brett to fight this one out alone — which is where the wildcard format came from in the first place.

The Great Brettini

Brett “The Great Brettini” Miller

Multiple Misters winner · Former captain of the Streets team
Handicap18

The golfer

One of the most decorated players ever to appear on the Misters Tour. Multiple wins, a captaincy behind him, a member at South Chesterfield and a society member at Birley, where he built a reputation for work around the greens — finesse, touch, and a consistency off the tee that took his handicap down into single figures.

That was then. A change in personal circumstances and living arrangements, combined with the founding of Green Machine, his contract cleaning company, has meant the business has thrived at the expense of the golf. The handicap has gone up, and up, and up. The scores have followed.

But the thing about Brettini is that it is still in there. The raw talent has not gone anywhere, and it does not need to be there for eighteen holes to matter. In a format where his best score on any given hole can be taken by whichever team has him that day, he only needs to produce it occasionally. He will make birdies. He may make an eagle — which, under the Birdie Club rules, would take the entire pot.

STRENGTHS

  • Genuine pedigree — multiple wins and a captaincy, and it does not disappear
  • Renowned around the greens for finesse and touch
  • Was consistent off the tee at his peak and the technique is still there
  • Off 18 with a game that used to be single figures — a dangerous combination
  • Format-proof: he only has to be good on the holes where it counts

WEAKNESSES

  • Rust. The business has come first and the scores show it
  • A handicap that has been rising rather than settling
  • No Misters 25 data to work from — an unknown quantity on current form
  • Playing with a different pairing every day, so no partnership rhythm

The man

Complex and multi-layered. A fierce competitor who wants to enjoy the time with the lads, though he sometimes enjoys that time more in the reflecting than in the living of it. A long-standing member of the tour and, in most people’s reckoning, one of its most decorated figures.

Dragon Tour outlook

The most consequential man on the tour, and the one nobody can predict. Under Brettini Performance Related Pay he earns a share of the winning team’s prize money in proportion to the number of holes on which his score was actually used — nothing at zero, half at nine, the full share at eighteen. The format has been designed to make his golf, not his presence, the thing that pays.

Every team wants him on the round where they are most likely to need a bail-out. Given that Nefyn holds stroke index 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 all on The Point, the Saturday is arguably the most valuable Brettini round of the three. That draw matters more than it looks.
